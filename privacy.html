Privacy notice

Effective October 25, 2026

This notice explains what information Rafael Arce Gazo, REALTOR® with DH Capital Realty (Nevada license S.0195953), collects through keyhouserafael.realtor and the open house sign-in page, and how it is used. It is provided under Nevada law (NRS 603A.340).

What I collect

  • What you type into a form: your name, email address, phone number, what you want to do (buy, sell, invest), your message, and, at open houses, whether you work with another agent.
  • Your choices on the permission boxes, with the date and the exact wording you agreed to.
  • Basic technical details sent with the form: the page address, how you found the site (for example a search engine or a campaign link), and your browser type.

How I use it

  • To answer your questions and help you buy, sell or invest in real estate.
  • To follow up with you by phone, text or email, within the permissions you give and the law.
  • If you checked the email box, to send listings, market updates and tips. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • If you checked the call and text box, to call and text you about real estate. Reply STOP to any text to opt out.

Who it is shared with

  • I do not sell your information, and I do not share it with other businesses for their own marketing.
  • My brokerage, DH Capital Realty, which supervises my work and may be required to keep transaction records.
  • Service providers that store or deliver information for me: Google (Google Sheets, Gmail and Apps Script store your details and send email) and GitHub (hosts this website).
  • People you ask me to work with during a transaction (for example a lender, escrow or title company, or inspector), and anyone the law requires.

Other companies on this site

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and may show a Google Calendar booking page. Google may receive your IP address and browser details when those load and may collect information about your online activity over time and across websites under its own privacy policy. This site does not use advertising trackers.

Your choices and rights

  • To see, correct or delete the information I have about you, email rafaelarcegazo@gmail.com or call (702) 335-4952. I will respond within 30 days.
  • To stop emails, use the unsubscribe link in any email. To stop calls and texts, reply STOP or tell me. I stop right away.
  • Nevada residents may send a request not to have their information sold to the email above. I do not sell information, but I will record your request.
  • If you ask me to delete your information, I keep only your email or phone number on a do-not-contact list so I do not contact you again by mistake.

How I protect it

Your information is kept in a private Google account protected by 2-Step Verification and is shared only with people who need it to help you.

Changes to this notice

If this notice changes, I will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. If the change is important, I will also tell you by email if you are on my list.

Fair housing

I am committed to the Fair Housing Act and Nevada fair housing law. I serve everyone equally, regardless of race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, national origin, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, or ancestry. Equal Housing Opportunity.
