Common questions

Quick answers to what buyers and sellers ask me most. Every situation is different, so call me for yours.

How much do I need for a down payment?

It depends on the loan. Some programs, like FHA loans, allow as little as 3.5% down, and VA loans can require nothing down for eligible veterans. A lender will confirm what you qualify for, and I can connect you with one.

Can I buy a home with an ITIN?

Yes, some lenders offer loans to buyers who use an ITIN instead of a Social Security number. Terms vary from lender to lender, so I'll help you find one who works with ITIN buyers.

How long does it take to buy a home?

The search takes as long as you need. Once your offer is accepted, escrow usually takes about 30 to 45 days, depending on your loan, the inspection, and the appraisal.

What does it cost to sell my home?

Common costs include agent commission, title and escrow fees, and any repairs or credits you agree to with the buyer. Commissions are negotiable. Before you list, I'll give you an estimate of what you'll take home.

Do I need to speak English to buy or sell?

No. The contracts are in English, but I explain every document and every step in Spanish so you know exactly what you're signing.
